# General YouTube thumbnail trends and best practices, 2025-2026 (all niches)

Source-quality legend used below: **[DATA]** = quantitative study with a stated method; **[CLAIM]** = a creator's or company's statement of its own results without published numbers; **[OPINION]** = expert or blog advice; **[OLD]** = pre-2025. Almost all quantitative studies are correlational (they describe what outliers look like) and none isolate cause.

## 1. What the best-known analysts say in 2025-2026 (Paddy Galloway, MrBeast, 1of10, vidIQ, Colin and Samir, ThumbnailTest and others)

### Takeaway
The two largest 2025-2026 datasets (1of10, about 300k videos from 2025; vidIQ, 500 breakouts from June 2026) agree that **little or no text** and **bright, high-contrast images** are common among outliers. On faces they partly disagree. Galloway and MrBeast's team frame the thumbnail as the starting point of the idea. For them the core job is a curiosity gap shown in a single, glanceable image, with the video then "confirming the click."

### Cited Findings
**1of10 "How to Go Viral on YouTube: Thumbnails and Titles" (published 6 Feb 2026, data on videos from 2025) [DATA]**
- Method: 300,000+ high-performing videos published in 2025, 62.6B views, 52,000 channels. "Outlier Score" = video views ÷ the channel's median views. No per-finding sample sizes or significance tests are given, and the results are correlational. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- 84% of thumbnails include text, and thumbnails with text got **about 19% fewer views**. The best setups had **no text, or fewer than 10 characters covering under 7% of the image**. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- Face vs. no face performs **similarly overall**. Multiple faces beat a single face. Faces meaningfully helped only channels with **200K+ subscribers**. Faces helped in Lifestyle/Vlogs, Finance and Beauty, and hurt in Health & Fitness and Movies & TV. Quote: "Faces amplify existing interest, they don't create it." — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- Colour and brightness: cyan-dominant thumbnails got about 36% more views than average, and the top dominant colours were cyan, green and yellow/orange. Views rise with brightness up to a "brightness score" of 100-110 (scale not defined). **Dark thumbnails consistently underperform.** — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- Titles from the same dataset: 5-word titles had the highest median engagement. Titles of 30 characters or fewer got about 60% more views, and titles near 70 characters did worst. Titles with numbers (35% of the sample) got about 11% fewer views. Negative titles (11% of the sample) got about 22% more views than positive ones. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- On how title and thumbnail relate (qualitative only): short titles "leave room" for the thumbnail, and thumbnail text should "clarify emotion, not explain the video." — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- In Dec 2025 vidIQ publicly claimed that removing your face could raise CTR. 1of10's Head of Content, Nate Curtiss, disputed this with the dataset above: "It depends on your niche, your format, and how many faces you use." SEJ's summary: the faces finding is "not dramatically different" on average, Finance does better with faces and Business worse. — [Search Engine Journal, 22 Dec 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/)

**1of10 "YouTube Thumbnail Trends in 2026" (Kyle du Randt, 19 Jun 2026) [OPINION, framed as outlier analysis, but no numbers shared]**
- Claimed trends: (1) hyper-saturated **three-colour palettes**, at least one of them neon or highly saturated, "more aggressive at the expense of elegance"; (2) face-forward emotional framing, with exaggerated expressions said to "dominate" many categories, including finance, tech and DIY; (3) **less text than in 2023**, with some entertainment, lifestyle, gaming and reaction outliers using none, while educational, tutorial, finance, legal and medical content still needs text; (4) AI-made thumbnails at "production parity", mainly as a way to test more variants. It also says thumbnails must work at **about 120 px wide**. No CTR figures, sample sizes or periods are given, and the page promotes 1of10's own thumbnail generator. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)
- **Conflict:** the "exaggerated expressions dominate" claim contradicts vidIQ's coded data (about 5% exaggerated, see below) and 1of10's own 2025 dataset (faces are neutral on average).

**vidIQ "YouTube Thumbnail Study: What 500 Breakout Thumbnails Reveal" (30 Jun 2026, updated 8 Jul 2026) [DATA, small sample, single month]**
- Method: 500 long-form (2+ min) "breakout" videos from the month ending 30 Jun 2026. Channels have 10k+ subscribers, videos have 50k+ views, there are at most 2 per channel, and every video earned at least 18x its channel's typical views. Each thumbnail was hand-coded for face, contrast, word count and expression, across 30 niches (no per-niche results published). — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- Face present: 69% of all 500, 75% of the top 100, 80% of the top 50. High contrast: 56% of all, 50% of the top 100, 46% of the top 50. Face, high contrast or both: 89%. — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- Text: 72% had overlay text, with a median of 5 words among those that did. Distribution: **no text 28%**, 1-3 words 24%, 4-5 words 16.8%, 6-7 words 12.6%, 8-9 words 6.8%, 10+ words 11.8%. — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- **Exaggerated expression: only about 5% of all breakouts (about 6% of the top 50).** In the top 50, the face often filled about one third of the frame against a strongly contrasting background with very few words. — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- vidIQ's derived design guidance (blog updated 14 Jul 2026) [OPINION based on the study]:
  - Text: 3-5 words in a large, heavy sans-serif, with a stroke, shadow or solid block behind it.
  - Face: about 1/4 to 1/3 of the frame, not colliding with the text.
  - One subject. Arrows or circles limited to one or two.
  - Avoid the lower-right corner (duration stamp).
  - **Avoid YouTube's own red, white and black palette** and prefer orange, teal, yellow or purple.
  - Keep key elements away from the edges, because mobile feeds may crop or zoom.
  - Shrink drafts to phone-feed size before publishing.
  - When testing, change one element per variant and read CTR and retention together.
  — [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-thumbnail-design-tips/)

**Paddy Galloway (interviewed by Colin and Samir; transcript page dated 1 Aug 2026, titled "The New Rules of YouTube (2025)") [OPINION from the best-known consultant]**
- "If they don't click, they don't watch." A thumbnail should be **"familiar but also unexpected"** so it stands out in a crowded feed. — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- He calls opening a **curiosity gap "the most important thing."** Method credited to MrBeast's team: start from the most exaggerated version of the packaging and pull it back to what you're comfortable with. Visual contrast works as a hook (his example: a split thumbnail of desert vs. green Sahara that makes the viewer think "no way"), and a proven contrast can become a repeatable packaging system. — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- Title and thumbnail **don't need to match exactly**. They work as one package, and the intro must **"confirm the click."** — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- He is against small corner logos ("It breaks up the image and adds busyness") and prefers subtle branding through text colour, font or clothing colour. — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- Many viewers don't know athletes' or celebrities' names, so **descriptors beat names** (e.g. "NFL player" instead of the name, "the Mozart of Gen Z"). — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- On process: write 30-40 title and thumbnail options per video and predict performance before upload. Elsewhere he advises "Ten Titles, Three Thumbnails, Every Video". — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited); [Creator Science podcast](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/paddy-galloway-2/)
- Older advice he still repeats [OLD]:
  - "Glance test": everything should be processable in a quick glance. Check whether title and thumbnail "compliment each other or contradict/repeat" (X, Jul 2024). — [X @PaddyG96](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1811083499044044951)
  - "3 element rule": the best thumbnails have 3 or fewer major elements, e.g. text, face, subject (X, 2021). — [X @PaddyG96](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1450463472907657220?lang=en-GB)
  - Make 3 thumbnail options per video, which only about 20-30% of channels really do (Jan 2024). — [X @PaddyG96](https://twitter.com/PaddyG96/status/1742221359613190312)
  - Interview summary: under 5 words of text, 1-3 focus areas, show action or the moment just before it rather than a posed smile, brighter is generally better, avoid busy backgrounds. — [Medium interview summary](https://medium.com/@ammarsheikhs/meet-the-godfather-of-youtube-strategy-paddy-galloway-interview-fcfc652bec2c)

**MrBeast "How to Succeed in MrBeast Production" (internal PDF leaked Sep 2024) [OLD; authenticity widely accepted but unconfirmed]**
- "The creative process for every video they produce starts with the title and thumbnail. These set the expectations for the viewer, and everything that follows needs to be defined with those in mind." On CTR: "This is what dictates what we do for videos." His example of a more clickable image: "An image of someone sitting in ketchup in a bathtub is exponentially more interesting." And: "If a viewer feels their expectations are not being matched, they'll click away." — [Simon Willison summary](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/)
- Rosanna Pansino published the PDF, and two former MrBeast producers told Passionfruit it looked legitimate. Not officially confirmed. — [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/leaked-document-allegedly-reveals-mrbeasts-secrets-to-youtube-success-the-key-takeaways/); [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/?p=81151)

**ThumbnailTest / Spotter / TubeBuddy / Think Media**
- I found no primary ThumbnailTest.com study with published aggregate results. Its pages are product and marketing comparisons. — [ThumbnailTest](https://thumbnailtest.com/guides/youtube-vs-thumbnailtest/)
- One secondhand figure (unverified, original not found): a "NoteLM" analysis of 127 A/B tests on 15 channels (Aug 2025-Jan 2026). 70% produced a clear winner with an average CTR lift of 37%, and 30% of tests of minor tweaks (e.g. colour tones) showed no difference. This was relayed by a search summary; treat it as unverified. Several testing guides make the same point: bold, clearly different variants are more likely to yield a winner. — [Epidemic Sound guide](https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/a-b-test-youtube-thumbnails/); [BerryViral](https://berryviral.com/blog/a-b-testing-youtube-thumbnails-what-actually-works-and-when-it-doesnt/)

### Inferences
- The best data points one way. **Fewer words** (ideally none or 1-3), **one bright, high-contrast focal image**, and **real faces with natural rather than extreme expressions** fit the current outlier pattern. The loud, neon style persists in some entertainment niches, per 1of10's trend blog, but it isn't supported by published numbers.
- Thumbnails without faces are viable (about 31% of vidIQ breakouts had no face, and 1of10 found no average penalty). This matters for channels that don't feature the creator.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 primary thumbnail content from Spotter, TubeBuddy, Think Media or ThumbnailTest with quantitative results. Colin and Samir appear only as interviewers of Galloway.
- Neither big study publishes per-niche results for documentary or history formats.

## 2. Is there a measurable trend toward fewer words, no text or a more "authentic" or documentary look? Text vs. no-text data

### Takeaway
Yes, for text: the two best datasets show text-free or very short text over-represented among outliers (1of10: text associated with about 19% fewer views; vidIQ: 28% no text and 24% with 1-3 words among breakouts). The "authentic or documentary look" trend is real in commentary but backed **only by opinion or vendor claims**, not data.

### Cited Findings
- 1of10, 2025 videos: text associated with about 19% fewer views, and the best setups had no text or fewer than 10 characters (under 7% of the area). [DATA] — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/)
- vidIQ, Jun 2026 breakouts: 28% had no text, 24% had 1-3 words, and the median among thumbnails with text was 5 words. 18.6% had 8+ words, so long text still appears in some breakouts. [DATA] — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- 1of10 trend piece: top performers use "less text than in 2023", but informational niches (education, finance, legal, medical) still need text. [OPINION] — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)
- One methodical-looking 2026 "study" found 3-5 words of large, high-contrast text outperformed no text in **search-driven** content, while dense text underperformed in browse and suggested. Its method and sample couldn't be verified. — [TubeAnalytics](https://www.tubeanalytics.net/resources/research/thumbnail-ctr-patterns-study-2026)
- Vendor blogs claim a 2025 shift toward "authentic, behind-the-scenes" aesthetics and say viewers are becoming "clickbait-blind". They describe the "neon explosion" era as cooling toward narrative-driven images or real screenshots with light grading. Figures like "165% higher engagement" come from AI-thumbnail vendors and are unsourced. [OPINION or marketing] — [Thumbnail AI blog](https://thumbnail-ai.app/blog/the-evolution-of-youtube-thumbnails-from-high-saturation-to-storytelling-2025-trends); [ThumbnailCreator](https://www.thumbnailcreator.com/blog/thumbnail-trends-leading-youtubers)
- **Contradiction:** 1of10's 2026 trend piece says palettes are getting **more** saturated and aggressive, not more muted. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)
- Galloway: "what is actually being shown in the thumbnail is so much more important than the design." The image or story matters more than graphic polish. [OPINION] — [Creator Science podcast](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/paddy-galloway-2/)

### Inferences
- "Authentic" seems to mean **real moments and real photos**, a single strong frame from the event, rather than desaturated or low-energy images. Brightness and contrast still correlate with views (1of10), so a documentary look should stay **bright and high-contrast**, not dark or moody.
- Text likely works best when the thumbnail is otherwise ambiguous, or when traffic is search-driven (informational content).

### Gaps
- No controlled, cross-channel text vs. no-text CTR experiment was found. All evidence is correlational outlier analysis.
- I found no data quantifying the "authentic or documentary look".

## 3. Faces: still effective? Are exaggerated expressions falling out of favour?

### Takeaway
Faces are still very common among breakouts (69-80% in vidIQ). On average, though, they don't beat no-face thumbnails (1of10), and they help mainly big channels and some niches. **Exaggerated expressions are rare among 2026 breakouts (about 5%)**, consistent with MrBeast's 2023 move to closed-mouth faces.

### Cited Findings
- vidIQ, Jun 2026: faces in 69% of breakouts and 80% of the top 50. Exaggerated expressions in about 5%. [DATA] — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- 1of10, 2025 videos: overall no difference between face and no face. Multi-face beats single-face, and faces help mainly at 200K+ subscribers. [DATA] — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/what-actually-makes-a-youtube-video-go-viral-in-2025/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/)
- MrBeast on X (Sep 2023): "I closed my mouth on all my thumbnails and the watch time went up on every video lol". This was after YouTube A/B testing; no numbers were given, and he made other changes at the same time (pacing, fewer cuts). [CLAIM, OLD] — [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/mrbeast-claims-closing-his-mouth-in-thumbnails-is-better-for-views/); [Digital Information World](https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2023/09/top-youtuber-mrbeast-proves-that.html)
- MKBHD's hypothesis: open-mouth faces may raise CTR, but more viewers feel clickbaited and leave early, so closed mouths win on watch time. [OPINION, OLD] — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/news-shocked-face-meta-over-fans-react-mrbeast-reveals-changing-youtube-thumbnail-style-might-boosted-views)
- Galloway: show action or the moment just before it, not a posed, smiling face looking at the camera. [OPINION, OLD] — [Medium interview summary](https://medium.com/@ammarsheikhs/meet-the-godfather-of-youtube-strategy-paddy-galloway-interview-fcfc652bec2c)
- The widely repeated "expressive faces +20-30% CTR (vidIQ)" figure traces to around 2022 and couldn't be found in a primary source. [OLD, unverified] — [vidIQ search context via SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/)
- Older academic support: a 2024 *Journal of Business Research* study of 16,215 YouTube covers found that strong sentiment in thumbnails (positive or negative) is associated with more views. Seen only as an abstract listing. [DATA, OLD] — [RePEc/JBR](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbrese/v183y2024ics0148296324003539.html)

### Inferences
- Emotion still matters, but the 2026 norm is **genuine or "real moment" emotion** rather than a soyface. For a channel without a host, the subject's own faces (players, people in the story) can serve the same function, and multiple faces correlate well (1of10).

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 data separating emotion intensity levels by CTR, beyond vidIQ's descriptive 5% figure.

## 4. Arrows, red circles and outlines or strokes around cut-out subjects

### Takeaway
I found **no 2025-2026 data** on arrows, circles or cut-out outlines. Current expert guidance tolerates them only sparingly (vidIQ: one or two at most). Commentary treats heavy use as dated or clickbait, but that is opinion.

### Cited Findings
- vidIQ (Jul 2026): one focal subject, with arrows or circles limited to one or two. Recommends a stroke, shadow or solid block **behind text** for legibility. [OPINION] — [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-thumbnail-design-tips/)
- vidIQ's 500-breakout study didn't code arrows, circles or outlines. — [vidIQ Research](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)
- 1of10's 2026 trends article doesn't mention arrows, circles or outlines. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)
- Long-standing viewer irritation with the "red arrow and circle epidemic" (forum discussion). Creators in the same thread report more traffic with arrows and circles. [OPINION, OLD] — [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/we-need-to-talk-about-the-clickbait-red-arrow-and-circle-epidemic.105421/)
- Vendor blogs describe a 2025 style of "real screenshots enhanced with colour grading, outlines, or subtle graphic elements". Outlines survive as a subtle separation tool. [OPINION] — [ThumbnailCreator](https://www.thumbnailcreator.com/blog/thumbnail-trends-leading-youtubers)
- Galloway's "3 element rule" and his dislike of corner logos ("adds busyness") argue against piling on graphic devices. [OPINION] — [X @PaddyG96](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1450463472907657220?lang=en-GB); [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)

### Inferences
- A single arrow or circle pointing to a detail the viewer would otherwise miss is still defensible. Stacked red arrows, circles and glowing outlines signal "clickbait" in the opinion of most commentators. YouTube's red is also discouraged, since it blends with the UI (vidIQ).

### Gaps
- No quantitative study of arrows, circles or outlines in 2025-2026 was found.

## 5. AI-generated images in thumbnails: trend, audience reaction, YouTube policy

### Takeaway
YouTube's official Help says using generative AI to create a thumbnail is **"production assistance" and does not require the altered/synthetic disclosure**. Thumbnails must still follow Community Guidelines and not mislead. Industry blogs say AI thumbnails are widespread, but I found no reliable CTR data or audience-reaction study.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube Help, "Disclosing use of altered or synthetic content": no disclosure needed for "using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline, script, thumbnail, title, or infographic" (listed under "Production assistance"). "Realistic AI content and meaningful changes require disclosure, while non-realistic or minor edits don't." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- YouTube Help, thumbnails: all custom thumbnails must follow Community Guidelines. They can be rejected for nudity or sexual content, hate, violence, or harmful and dangerous content, and repeat violations can remove custom-thumbnail privileges for 30 days. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en)
- YouTube's "egregious clickbait" enforcement (announced Dec 2024, starting in India): videos whose title or thumbnail promises something the video doesn't deliver (e.g. a "the president resigned!" claim) are removed, initially without a strike. The focus is breaking news and current events, and the detection method is undisclosed. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/18/youtube-says-it-will-start-cracking-down-on-videos-with-clickbait-titles-in-india); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/12/19/youtube-clickbait-crackdown-india-egregious-misleading-videos/amp/)
- Third-party write-ups say the spam and deceptive-practices policy still bans misleading thumbnails regardless of how they were made. They also report a January 2026 enforcement wave against mass-produced content under a renamed "inauthentic content" policy. Both are secondary sources. — [Pikzels](https://pikzels.com/blog/are-ai-thumbnails-allowed-on-youtube); [AI Metadata Cleaner](https://aimetadatacleaner.com/blog/youtube-ai-content-identification-policies-2026)
- 1of10 (Jun 2026): AI thumbnails are at "production parity" and appear among top-CTR channels; the main advantage is testing more variants. No data, and 1of10 sells an AI thumbnail generator. [OPINION, conflict of interest] — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)

### Inferences
- From a policy standpoint, AI-made thumbnails are allowed without a label. The practical risk is **misrepresentation**: a realistic AI scene that never happened in the video risks the clickbait policy and viewer trust. Documentary channels that care about credibility have extra reason to use real frames.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025-2026 data on CTR or audience sentiment toward visibly AI-generated thumbnails. The available sources are vendors with conflicts of interest.
- I couldn't confirm whether the "egregious clickbait" enforcement has expanded beyond India.

## 6. Mobile legibility: display sizes, squint test, minimum text size, official specs, safe areas

### Takeaway
YouTube Help now recommends **3840×2160 (16:9), minimum width 640 px**, with a 2 MB limit on mobile upload and 50 MB on desktop. 1280×720 is still the community "working" standard. Real display sizes are small, roughly 120-370 px wide depending on surface, which is why "glance test" design (few elements, big shapes) matters. Keep the **bottom-right corner clear** for the duration badge.

### Cited Findings
- Official specs: recommended "3840 x 2160 pixels for videos" and "2160 x 3840 for Shorts"; "a minimum width of 640 pixels"; JPG or PNG; 2 MB limit on mobile and 50 MB on desktop; aim for 16:9. "Vertical videos with 16:9 custom thumbnails will be replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en)
- vidIQ (Jul 2026) still lists 1280×720, minimum 640 px, under 2 MB, and says PNG keeps text sharpest. It also warns that mobile feeds may crop or zoom 16:9 thumbnails, so faces and text should stay off the edges. Avoid the lower-right corner (timestamp). — [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-thumbnail-design-tips/)
- 1of10: thumbnails need to work at **about 120 px wide**, and most YouTube traffic is mobile (no figure given). — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026/)
- Community estimates of rendered sizes (not official, and inconsistent across sources):
  - Desktop home: about 360×202. Desktop suggested sidebar: about 168×94.
  - Mobile feed: about full screen width. Mobile suggested: about 168×94.
  - Another guide suggests testing at about 246×138.
  - Safe-area recommendations: roughly a central 1100×620 box on 1280×720. Timestamp exclusion estimates range from about 120×60 to about 150×100 px.
  - YouTube publishes no official thumbnail safe area.
  — [Klap](https://klap.app/blog/thumbnail-size-for-youtube-videos); [Thumix](https://www.thumix.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-safe-zone); [Thumbcrafted](https://thumbcrafted.com/guide/youtube-thumbnail-safe-zones)
- Galloway's "glance test": can you process everything in a quick glance? Viewers give milliseconds while scrolling. [OPINION, OLD] — [X @PaddyG96](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1811083499044044951)
- Galloway (2025-26): some packaging is designed for TV viewing, and YouTube is often watched on TVs. — [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)

### Inferences
- A practical check: preview at about 120-170 px wide (the suggested and sidebar size). The subject and any words should still read there, which effectively forces 1-3 big words and one subject.
- Uploading at 1280×720 or higher is fine. 3840×2160 is now the official recommendation, but the extra size likely matters mainly for TV.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube figures for rendered thumbnail sizes per surface (home, suggested, search, TV) or for a minimum text size were found.
- TV-specific sizes were not found.

## 7. Curiosity gap and how title and thumbnail should complement (not repeat) each other; how styles changed 2023 → 2026

### Takeaway
There is consensus among the top strategists that the title and thumbnail are one package. They should **complement rather than repeat** each other, together opening a curiosity gap that the video's intro immediately "confirms". From 2023 to 2026 the measurable shift is toward less text, closed or natural faces over shock faces, and brighter, simpler single-subject images. The colour direction is disputed: more saturated per 1of10's trend piece, more natural or documentary per vendor commentary.

### Cited Findings
- Galloway: check whether title and thumbnail "compliment each other or contradict/repeat" (Jul 2024) [OLD]. In 2025-26: they don't need to match exactly, a curiosity gap is "the most important thing", and the intro must "confirm the click". — [X @PaddyG96](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1811083499044044951); [Transcript Desk](https://thetranscriptdesk.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-youtube-2025-edited)
- 1of10: short titles leave room for the thumbnail, and thumbnail text should "clarify emotion, not explain the video". ### Inferences
- A practical division of labour that fits all sources: the **thumbnail shows the moment or the emotion** (one image, 0-3 words that add tension rather than restate the title). The **title supplies the context or the question** (about 5 words, under 30-50 characters). Neither should repeat the other's words.

### Gaps
- No 2023 baseline dataset with the same method exists, so the "2023 → 2026" change is inferred from different studies and statements rather than measured longitudinally.
