# YouTube's official guidance and tools for thumbnails (2025-2026), with a focus on Test & Compare

Research date: 2026-11-09. Primary sources are YouTube Help Center pages and blog.youtube posts. Trade press (Search Engine Journal, Social Media Today, TechCrunch, ppc.land) is used for dates and quotes. The Help Center pages carry no "last updated" date, so the numbers below describe the pages as read on 2026-11-09.

## 1. Test & Compare (A/B testing of thumbnails and titles): how it works today

### Takeaway
Test & Compare (Help Center name: "A/B test titles & thumbnails") lets any channel with advanced features test up to 3 thumbnails, 3 titles, or 3 title + thumbnail combinations on a long-form video. It runs only from YouTube Studio on desktop. The winner is picked by **watch time (watch time share / watch time per impression), not CTR**. Tests last from a few days up to two weeks, and the possible results are Winner, Performed Same and Inconclusive. Small channels or videos with few impressions often get "Inconclusive".

### Cited Findings
**Timeline**
- June 2023: YouTube announced it was developing thumbnail A/B testing and began a limited test with selected creators. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-title-testing-youtube-studio/753015/); [TechCrunch, 12/06/2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/12/youtube-creators-can-test-multiple-video-thumbnails)
- June 2024: "Thumbnail Test & Compare" began rolling out to all channels with advanced features, on desktop Studio. — [YouTube Blog, Rene's Top Five 14/06/2024](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-june-14-2024/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-rolls-out-thumbnail-a-b-testing-to-all-channels/519281/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/12/youtube-creators-can-test-multiple-video-thumbnails)
- July 2025: YouTube started experimenting with **title** testing for a "small percentage of creators". Quote: "We're starting to experiment with a feature that gives creators the ability to test titles as well as thumbnails". — [Social Media Today, 14/07/2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-title-testing-youtube-studio/753015/); [Tubefilter, 16/07/2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/07/16/youtube-feature-test-and-compare-titles-thumbnails/)
- September 2025 (Made on YouTube): title A/B testing was announced alongside thumbnail testing. — [Search Engine Journal, 04/12/2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/)
- December 4, 2025: title A/B testing rolled out **globally** to every creator with advanced features, announced on the Creator Insider channel. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/)
- September 23, 2026 (Made on YouTube 2026, Aparna Pappu, VP of YouTube): YouTube says A/B testing "officially launched in 2024" and that creators have run **more than 40 million A/B experiments on titles and thumbnails since 2024**. It also announced, as "coming soon", testing of up to 3 video **cuts** to see which hook works best. — [YouTube Blog, Made on YouTube 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-new-tools-power-creation-journey/)

**Current rules (Help Center)**
- "Creators can test and compare up to 3 different titles and thumbnails." Options: titles only, thumbnails only, or title + thumbnail together. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- Desktop only ("only available on computers through YouTube Studio"). Requires advanced features (phone + ID or video verification, or channel history) and following the Community Guidelines, or access can be lost. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)
- Not eligible: Shorts, scheduled Lives, Premieres (eligible once they end and become long-form), made-for-kids videos, mature-audience videos, private videos. Tests on age-restricted videos do not run. Live archives can be tested. Once a video becomes a Short, its tests are lost. — [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- Metric: the option with the "highest watch time will be shown to all viewers". YouTube chose watch time over CTR, describing itself as "optimizing for overall watch time over other metrics like CTR" to favour quality engagement. It argues that a good title and thumbnail help viewers understand what the video is about so they don't waste time on videos they don't want. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/)
- YouTube shows the variants "evenly" across viewers and picks the winner "based on which one generates the most watch time share". — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-adds-title-testing-youtube-studio/753015/). SEJ describes the metric as "watch time per impression". — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/)
- Duration: tests should finish "within two weeks". They can take from a few days up to two weeks depending on impressions and how recently the video was published. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)
- Results:
  - **Winner**: one option clearly beat the others on watch time share, with statistical significance.
  - **Performed Same**: the options performed about equally, so the creator picks.
  - **Inconclusive**: "There was no strong statistical difference in engagement between your options". The first title and thumbnail uploaded become the default.
  - Source: [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en). Older versions and some third-party guides mention a "Preferred" result (likely better, without enough confidence). The current page no longer lists it. — [Help 13861714 (search snippet)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- Small channels: the page doesn't mention them by name. It does say that a lack of impressions can prevent a winner from being declared, and that videos with more views are more likely to produce one. It recommends **testing older videos first** to limit the effect on the channel's overall views. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)
- Control group: a small percentage of viewers may see only the default thumbnail and title, and their data is excluded from the calculation. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)
- Changing the title or thumbnail during a test stops it, and it has to be started again. If any thumbnail is below 720p (1280x720), **all** test thumbnails are downscaled to 480p. Results can vary between runs, and third-party tools may reach different conclusions. — [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- The creator can override the chosen option at any time from the metadata editor. — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/)
- Shorts: custom Shorts thumbnails arrived in July 2026, **without A/B testing**. — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/); [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)

**What comes next: "dynamic thumbnails" (Made on YouTube, 23/09/2026)**
- In Ask Studio, creators will be able to "use dynamic thumbnails to have YouTube recommend the best of three options to different segments of your audience". Instead of a single winner for everyone, a different thumbnail can go to each audience segment. Ask Studio will also be able to review old videos in the background and "suggest and test" refreshed thumbnails. No date or availability was given. — [YouTube Blog, Made on YouTube 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-new-tools-power-creation-journey/)

### Inferences
- Because the metric is watch time and not CTR, a "shocking" thumbnail that gets more clicks but attracts the wrong viewers can lose the test. For a documentary channel, the thumbnail has to promise exactly what the video delivers.
- A new channel with few impressions will very likely get "Inconclusive" or "Performed Same". Testing makes more sense once a video has steady traffic (YouTube's own advice is to test older videos first).
- "Dynamic thumbnails" suggests YouTube is moving from "one winning thumbnail" to "a thumbnail per audience segment", so having 3 truly different versions (not tiny variations) gains value.

### Gaps
- YouTube has not published aggregate results (for example, average watch time lift from tests). The only figure is the count of more than 40 million experiments.
- No official minimum number of impressions or views needed for a test to be conclusive.
- It is not officially documented which surfaces the test runs on (Home, Search, Suggested, TV), or whether external traffic counts.
- I couldn't confirm the exact availability date of dynamic thumbnails.

## 2. Thumbnail specs and how thumbnails display

### Takeaway
The current Help Center page (read November 2026) recommends **3840x2160 (4K), 16:9, minimum width 640 px**, in JPG/PNG-type formats. The size limit is **2 MB from mobile and 50 MB from desktop**. This replaces the classic advice of "1280x720, maximum 2 MB" (my training data; the change date isn't on the page). Custom Shorts thumbnails at 9:16 have existed for YPP members since July 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Long-form video: recommended 3840x2160 px, minimum width 640 px, 16:9 ratio. Shorts: recommended 2160x3840 px, minimum height 640 px, 9:16. Podcast playlists: 1:1. — [YouTube Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)
- Formats: images such as JPG or PNG. Size: from mobile, 2 MB (video) and 10 MB (podcasts). From desktop, 50 MB (video, Shorts and podcasts). — [YouTube Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)
- Uploading custom thumbnails requires a verified account. Without verification, only the automatic suggestions are available. There is a daily upload limit that varies by country and channel history ("Daily custom thumbnail limit reached" means trying again in 24 hours). — [YouTube Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)
- Vertical videos with a 16:9 thumbnail get an **automatically generated 4:5 thumbnail** on Home, Explore and Subscriptions. The custom one still appears in the watch feed, history and non-mobile platforms. YouTube may turn off custom thumbnails in some searches it considers inappropriate. — [YouTube Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)
- Test & Compare: thumbnails below 1280x720 drag the whole test down to 480p. — [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- July 24, 2026: YPP creators can upload custom thumbnails for Shorts, and on desktop can choose from 3 suggested frames. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-custom-thumbnail-updates/)
- Display: YouTube redesigned the desktop home page with **larger thumbnails and fewer videos per row**. Todd Beaupré (Creator Insider, interviewed by Rene Ritchie, 2026) said it "actually drove more attention and engagement around long-form" and "We wouldn't have launched that change if it reduced viewership". Android Authority called it a test in April 2025. The larger mobile thumbnails are tied to the October 2024 update. — [Search Engine Journal, 02/09/2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-bigger-thumbnails-long-form-engagement/588102/)
- The thumbnail competes against other videos on Home, "Up Next", search results and subscriptions. — [YouTube Help 7628154](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154)

### Inferences
- Exporting at 3840x2160 (or at least 1920x1080) as JPG/PNG is safe. Below 1280x720 is risky (Test & Compare penalises it). If uploading from the phone, keep it under 2 MB.
- Larger home-page thumbnails make detail and faces more readable, but the thumbnail still has to work small (search, suggested, mobile).

### Gaps
- No official YouTube source found on the **position of the duration timestamp** (bottom right is the convention, but I didn't verify it in official documentation), nor on safe zones or TV display.
- No official documentation found on hover autoplay previews ("inline playback") and how they interact with the thumbnail and with the impression metric.
- No exact date found for when the recommendation changed from 1280x720 / 2 MB to 3840x2160 / 50 MB on desktop.

## 3. Policies: thumbnail policy, clickbait crackdown, AI disclosure, YouTube's AI tools

### Takeaway
The thumbnail policy bans sexual content, shocking violence, gore, vulgar language and thumbnails that **mislead about what the video contains**. Since December 2024 YouTube has enforced against "egregious clickbait" (a title or thumbnail that promises something the video doesn't deliver, especially news), starting in India, with removal and no strike at first. I found no official confirmation that it has expanded to other countries. The AI disclosure requirement does **not** apply to AI-made thumbnails (explicitly exempt). In 2026 YouTube launched AI thumbnail generation in Ask Studio.

### Cited Findings
**Thumbnail policy**
- Banned (the list isn't complete): "Pornographic imagery"; sexual acts or fetishes; "Nudity, including genitals"; "unwanted sexualization"; "Violent imagery that intends to shock or disgust"; "Graphic or disturbing imagery with blood or gore"; "Vulgar or lewd language"; "A thumbnail that misleads viewers to think they're about to view something that's not in the video". Impersonation is also banned (copying another channel's branding, or "using AI to copy the voice or likeness of an individual"). — [YouTube Help 9229980](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980)
- Borderline cases: "we may age-restrict the video, or we may remove the thumbnail, but we don't issue a strike". Other violations: the thumbnail is removed and a strike may be issued. Pornography can lead to channel termination. The first violation is usually a warning (it expires after 90 days with policy training). Three strikes in 90 days can mean termination. — [YouTube Help 9229980](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9229980)
- Repeated violations can remove custom thumbnail privileges for 30 days. — [YouTube Help 72431](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)

**"Egregious clickbait" (December 2024)**
- Post on the Google India blog, December 18, 2024. Definition: egregious clickbait "occurs when the video's title or thumbnail includes promises or claims that aren't delivered within the video itself", especially for breaking news or current events. Examples: the title "the president resigned!" on a video that doesn't cover it, or a thumbnail saying "top political news" on a video with no news. Enforcement: removal, initially "without issuing a strike". Priority on new uploads. "slowly rolling this out in India over the coming months". — [Google India Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/strengthening-enforcement-against-egregious-clickbait-on-youtube/); [TechCrunch 18/12/2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/18/youtube-says-it-will-start-cracking-down-on-videos-with-clickbait-titles-in-india); [Tubefilter 19/12/2024](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/12/19/youtube-clickbait-crackdown-india-egregious-misleading-videos/)
- A spokesperson said it would expand to more countries "in the coming months". — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91252550/youtube-has-a-new-plan-to-combat-clickbait); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/amp/youtube-declares-war-against-egregious-clickbait-thumbnails-and-titles-in-india/)

**AI: disclosure and YouTube's own tools**
- The altered/synthetic content disclosure policy lists as **not** requiring disclosure: "using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline, script, thumbnail, title, or infographic". Disclosure is required for realistic content that shows a real person saying or doing something they didn't, alters footage of a real event or place, or creates a realistic scene that didn't happen. YouTube may apply the label automatically (content from its own GenAI tools, C2PA metadata, or detection). — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)
- July 24, 2026: generative thumbnails in **Ask Studio** for long-form videos ("Simply ask for a thumbnail for your latest video", then adjust colors and layout by chat, "tailored to your video's specific themes and to your specific style"). The post doesn't mention AI labeling. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-studio-custom-thumbnail-updates/). ppc.land reports it as available globally, desktop only. — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-ends-2-year-wait-for-shorts-thumbnails-but-blocks-a-b-testing/)
- TubeBuddy reports that Ask Studio thumbnails carry an identifier marking them as AI-generated (third-party source, not verified with YouTube). — [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-ai-thumbnail-generator/)
- Background: YouTube previewed AI thumbnails at Made on YouTube in September 2024. — [TechCrunch 18/09/2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/18/youtube-studio-now-lets-creators-brainstorm-video-ideas-with-the-help-of-ai)
- September 23, 2026: Ask Studio will generate "channel-matched thumbnails", dynamic thumbnails across 3 options, and suggestions for refreshing thumbnails on old videos. — [YouTube Blog, Made on YouTube 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-new-tools-power-creation-journey/)

### Inferences
- For a channel about historical football matches, the clickbait risk comes from claims or images that aren't in the video (for example, a player who doesn't appear, or a result that doesn't happen). The rule is "the thumbnail promises, the video delivers".
- An AI-generated or AI-retouched thumbnail does not, by policy, require the video to be labeled. Even so, a photorealistic image of a scene that never happened could fall under "misleading" in the thumbnail policy or under impersonation if it uses a real person's likeness.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation (blog or Help Center) found that the egregious clickbait enforcement has reached countries beyond India, nor any results figures. A third-party blog claims changes in April 2026 that I couldn't verify.
- Not documented whether Ask Studio thumbnails carry SynthID or C2PA, or whether they trigger the automatic label on the video.

## 4. What YouTube says about CTR, watch time and good thumbnails

### Takeaway
YouTube's official line: CTR on its own is not the goal. About half of channels sit between 2% and 10%. CTR naturally drops when impressions grow (Home). "High CTR + low average view duration" is the official sign of clickbait. The system optimizes for satisfaction and watch time ("not all watch time is created equal"), and Test & Compare uses watch time on purpose.

### Cited Findings
- "Half of all channels and videos on YouTube have an impressions CTR that can range between 2% and 10%." Newer channels and videos with few views can fall outside that range. Impressions from external websites and end screens don't count. — [YouTube Help 7628154](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154); [Creator Studio Help](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/7628154?hl=en)
- A thumbnail may be clickbait if it shows "high CTR but low average view duration and lower than expected Impressions". Clickbait videos are recommended less. When a video gets many impressions (for example from Home), "it's natural for the CTR to be lower". The page advises against "using thumbnails or titles that are clickbait" to raise CTR. — [YouTube Help 7628154](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154)
- The Test & Compare metric is watch time over CTR, "optimizing for overall watch time over other metrics like CTR", because it "will best inform our creators content strategy decisions". — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/); [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)
- Todd Beaupré (Senior Director of Growth & Discovery), Creator Insider "YouTube Algorithm 2026 - What Creators Need to Know", with Rene Ritchie, around September 1, 2026: "probably all your videos have about maybe a 10% or less click-through rate"; "We don't want to just assume that all time is valuable"; "No metric on its own is a good indicator of value"; metric weighting "doesn't even work such that it's the same for you at all times". On the home page redesign: "often less is more". — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-bigger-thumbnails-long-form-engagement/588102/)
- Rene Ritchie (Creator Liaison), quoted from a transcript of a Peter McKinnon video: "The thumbnail makes a promise and the video delivers on that promise"; "not all watch time is created equal". Secondary source, not verified against the original video. — [Rosetta transcript](https://rosetta.to/u/petermckinnon/youtube-isn-t-the-problem-it-s-you?t=0)
- YouTube's argument for the Test & Compare metric: good titles and thumbnails help the viewer understand what the video is about and not waste time on videos they don't want. — [YouTube Help 16391400](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)

### Inferences
- For a 9-minute documentary, a thumbnail that brings the "right" viewer (someone who stays) is worth more than one that maximizes clicks. A drop in CTR as the video reaches Home is normal and not something to fix.
- Thumbnail/content consistency is rewarded three ways: the Test & Compare metric, fewer recommendations for clickbait, and the egregious clickbait policy.

### Gaps
- No primary source (official YouTube video or post) found with direct Rene Ritchie quotes on CTR vs watch time. The available quotes are secondhand.
- No official YouTube Culture & Trends material found on thumbnail design (faces, text, colours). YouTube publishes no visual design guide beyond the policy and specs. Design guidance comes from creators and third parties.
- I didn't watch the full Creator Insider video from September 2026. Quotes come through ppc.land and SEJ.
